WRITING NOTES

Cover Letters

  • what do you want your letter to accomplish? Decide on one or two key points to convey
  • i.e. focus on key points in your resume (if applicable)
  • Sell Yourself (Why are you a good fit?)
  • Opening paragraph
    • state purpose
    • give person reason to read on
  • Body:
    • Meat of Letter
    • 2-3 Paragraphs
    • Sell yourself, i.e. why are you the right person for the job?
    • What does it take to do the job?
    • What can you do for the company?
    • Explain your experience, what can you bring to the table?
