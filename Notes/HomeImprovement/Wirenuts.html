Wirenuts

This document covers the right way to use wirenuts
  • strip 1" off end of wires
  • twist wires together
    • some wirenuts advertise that you don't need to twist the wires together beforehand. Do it anyway.
    • use a pair of blunt nose ("linesman") pliers & carefully twist the wires tightly & neatly
    • if necessary, trim the resulting end
  • twist the wirenut on. there should be no exposed bare wire.
