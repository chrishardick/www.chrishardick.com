3 Way Switches

I. Basic Operation

  • The purpose of a 3 Way Switch is to control a light (or receptacle) from 2 locations (a 4 way switch controls a light from 3 or more locations).
  • A 3 Way Switch has three poles. One common & two travelers; the common pole is usually black (or marked in some other way as different)
