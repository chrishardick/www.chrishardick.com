Synchronous vs. Asynchronous

The basic idea is that synchronous operations are operations in which you wait for completion, and while you're waiting, no useful work can be performed. Asynchronous operations are operations which you do not have to wait for completion, they return immediately, and when processing is complete (depending on the operation), they may or may not notify you of completion.

Synchronous/Asynchronous Function Calls

Normally, when you make a function call, you have to wait for it to complete processing before it returns (synchronous function).
