TIRE ROTATION

  • Directional Tires: must remain on the same side of the car. They're rotated from front to back & vice versa. Directional tires have a rotation arrow on the side-wall. They have "swept back" threads for better water traction 
ROTATION (NON-DIRECTIONAL TIRES)
================================

BEFORE        AFTER
======        =====

LF  RF        RR  LR


LR  RR        LF  RF
HERE'S HOW I DO IT W/O LIFTING ENTIRE CAR @ ONCE.
  1. LIFT ONE SIDE OF CAR & SWITCH TIRES (FRONT TO BACK, BACK TO FRONT)
  2. LIFT THE OTHER SIDE & REPEAT STEP #1
  3. NOTE: YOU'RE DONE W/ BACK OF CAR. BUT FRONT OF CAR NEEDS TO BE REVERSED.
  4. LIFT FRONT OF CAR & SWITCH TIRES (LEFT TO RIGHT, RIGHT TO LEFT)
  5. YOU'RE DONE
