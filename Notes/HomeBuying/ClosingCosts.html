CLOSING COSTS
GENERALLY 2-5% OF PURCHASE PRICE

I. Loan Origination Fees (Points) & Other Loan Charges

  • Points: 0-2% of amount borrowed
  • Other Loan Charges
    • 1% of amount borrowed
    • appraisals
    • obtaining credit report
    • preparing document
    • processing application

II. Escrow Fees

  • $200 - $1,000 - depending on purchase price

III. Homeowner's Insurance

  • Lenders usually require you to pay 1st year's premium @ closing
  • $200 - $3,000 - depending on value of home & amount of coverage you want

IV. Title Insurance

  • $200 - $1,000 - depending on purchase price

V. Property Tax

  • You may have to reimburse the seller for any property tax they paid in advance

VI. Legal Fees

  • $0 - $2,000 - depending on state you live in (pretty sure NYS requires real estate lawyer)

VII. Inspections

  • $200 - $500

VIII. Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)

  • Required if you put less than 20% down

IX. Prepaid Loan Interest

  • Interest that accrues from the date your loan is funded (usually one business day before the escrow closes) up to 30 days prior to your first regularly scheduled loan payment
  • You can work it out w/ lender such that you do not have to pay any advance loan interest
