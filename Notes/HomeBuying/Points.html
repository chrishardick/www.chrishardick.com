POINTS

  • Up front interest charges
  • Tax Deductible
  • 1 point = 1% of amount you're borrowing
  • Interest rate & # of points have inverse relationship. I.e more in points = less in annual interest rate
  • No point loans usually only make sense when you're strapped on cash for the closing (because you're annual interest rate is usually less - but ALWAYS DO THE MATH)
  • Points or No Points: Always do the math - Divide Dollar Amount by Monthly Savings to determine # months to break even
