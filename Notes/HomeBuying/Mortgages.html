MORTGAGES

MORTGAGE ORIGINATORS

  • Banks: Smaller banks usually have the better rates
  • Savings-and-loan associations: smaller savings-and-loans usually have better rates
  • Mortgage bankers: only do mortgages - best ones offer very competitive rates

WHETHER OR NOT TO HIRE A MORTGAGE BROKER

  • typically charge 0.5% - 2%
  • ask the mortgage broker what their take is. Negotiate a better rate
  • how many lenders does the broker do business with

TIPS

  • Ask your agent & property inspector if there are any aspects of the property which may give a lender cause for concern (i.e. to deny a mortgage)
