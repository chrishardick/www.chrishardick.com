C++ - Stream Buffers and Streams

Stream Buffers

  • Represent I/O devices
  • Provide a low level interface for unformatted I/O to a device
  • Low Level, Raw Bytes

Streams

  • Provide a higher level wrapper around Stream Buffers via unformatted I/O and formatted
  • Stream objects may also manage a stream buffer's lifetime
  • High level (e.g. text, integers, etc.)
