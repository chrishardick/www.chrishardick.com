Operator Precedence

From highest to lowest (operators on the same line, have the same priority):
  • postfix expr++, expr--
  • unary ++expr, --expr, -expr, ~, !
  • multiplicative *, /, %
  • additive +, -
  • shift <<, >>, >>>
  • relational <, >, <=, >=, instanceof
  • equality ==, !=
  • bitwise AND &
  • bitwise exclusive OR ^
  • bitwise OR |
  • logical AND &&
  • logical OR ||
  • ternary ? :
  • assignment =+=-=, +=, *=, /=, %=, &= , ^=, |=, <<=, &rt;&rt;=, &rt;&rt;&rt;=
