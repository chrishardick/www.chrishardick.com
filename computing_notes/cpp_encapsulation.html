C++ Encapsulation

  • Encapsulation in C++ (or OOP in general) is protecting your data.
  • The basic idea is that you only want to expose your public interface
  • Everything else should not be accessible to the general public
  • Encapsulation (as an OOP term) is also known as 'data hiding'
  • Encapsulation is not just an OO term, it's a quintessential programming topic
  • It deals with creating strong interfaces to your data
  • Whether you're in C, C++, Perl, whatever, the general principal of encapsulation should be followed
