Sockets - Port Numbers

  • Port numbers are 16 bit unsigned integers
  • 2^16 = 65,536 Ports (0 - 65,535)
  • Servers normally use their well-known port numbers
  • Clients normally use ephemeral port numbers (short lived port #'s assigned by the kernel)
  • IANA (Internet Assigned Numbers Authority) www.iana.org maintains a list of port number assignments
  • IANA divides the port numbers into 3 ranges
    • well-known ports
      • 0-1023
      • Controlled & assigned by IANA
      • In UNIX, these are reserved ports which require superuser privileges
    • reserved ports
      • 1024-49151
      • Not controlled by IANA, but IANA maintans a list as a convenience to the community
      • Berkeley derived implementations ephemeral ports are from 1024-5000
      • Solaris ephemeral ports are from 32768 to 65535
      • Therefore, for reasons of portability, choose a port from 5001 to 32767
    • dynamic or private ports
      • 49152-65535
      • ephemeral ports
