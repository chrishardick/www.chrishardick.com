NoSQL - Introduction

  • Generally more flexible than SQL systems
  • Usually a key with a flexible amount of data
  • Many NoSQL implementations compromise consistency in favor of availability.
  • NoSQL databases generally offer a concept of eventual consistency - in which changes are gradually propagated to all nodes

Wide Column Store

  • A wide column store is a type of NoSQL database.
  • It involves tables, rows and columns
  • However, the names and the columns can vary from row to row.
